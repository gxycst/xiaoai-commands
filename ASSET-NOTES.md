# 私人人物图片

使用内置 ImageGen 进行透明背景抠图；未调用 Fal。源照片与抠图不上传公开仓库。

最终提示词：

```text
Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied photograph of the girl. Create a clean photographic cutout of ONLY this same girl's head, hair, neck, shoulders and upper chest, cropped just below the top of her white dress. Preserve her exact facial identity, expression, hair ties, real skin texture, white dress and photographic appearance. Remove sofa, panda bedding, keyboard, wires and all background completely. Actual transparent alpha background, not a checkerboard. Head and shoulders centered, tight square framing with small margin, natural silhouette, clean delicate hair edges. Do not cartoonize or change her face, do not add objects or text, do not include lower torso or hands. Asset intended for a small friendly child-themed website logo; photo cutout only, speaker will be a separate vector icon.
```

Logo 在页面中由人物 PNG 和原生 SVG 音箱组合。照片单独保存为现有 skills 目录下的 niuniu.png；SVG 随代码交付。
